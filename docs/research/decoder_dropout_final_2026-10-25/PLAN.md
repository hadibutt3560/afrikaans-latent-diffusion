# Final Afrikaans decoder-dropout experiment

Fixed on 25 October 2026 before the run, this exploratory follow-up to job 74177 tested whether decoder dropout of 0.1 during training (D1) improved held-out reconstruction over an otherwise identical full-decoder adaptation without dropout (D0), both fine-tuning all 12 mT5 decoder blocks from the original autoencoder (job 56531, step 30500) with the same data order (seed 20261024 + epoch) and at most 26,620 updates per arm. Only D1 could be the candidate, requiring at least 70.7005325922 chrF (B0 + 3), at least 14/81 exact reconstructions and at least 1 chrF above the best eligible D0 on the 81 development passages. On the 103-passage reserve, success against B0 required +3 chrF with a 95% interval above 0 (10,000 paired document-bootstrap replicates), an exact-match increase of at least 0.03 and the numeric and degeneracy safeguards, with D1 against D0 assessed separately.

The plan also set the 16-hour budget, admission and stopping rules, preflight checks, the conditional generation comparison on 256 shared latents (seeds 20261024–20261279) with its regression guards, and the export contents. The full plan as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/decoder_dropout_final_2026-10-25/PLAN.md.
