# Joint reconstruction adaptation — approved 25 October 2026

## Motivation and limits

LD4LG (Lovelace et al., NeurIPS 2023), sections 3.1 and appendix E, trains compression and reconstruction modules before diffusion; its mT5 translation experiment jointly adapts the backbone and autoencoding modules. Paper: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/b2a2bd5d5051ff6af52e1ef60aefd255-Paper-Conference.pdf . This motivates testing joint reconstruction, but does not demonstrate that this restricted Afrikaans intervention will work. It is not a paper replication. Original job56531 trained both Perceiver components and the final language-decoder block; jobs74177 and75003 froze both Perceiver components while adapting the language decoder. Their negative/mixed results do not locate the cause of fidelity loss.

## Single intervention

D0: all 12 mT5 decoder blocks and final layer norm trainable. D1: identical plus the complete `perceiver_ae.perceiver_decoder` reconstruction mapping. Both start fresh from original job56531/step30500, SHA256 123f4508e4aa26967c05a2ad413cebb8f2a2b90fc2420dab757704495dc27f5c. This preserves cached 32x64 norm8 latents: no cache regeneration or diffusion retraining. Gradients must flow through the reconstruction mapping in D1; D0 mapping must remain bitwise unchanged. Explicit runtime assertions cover this and unchanged encoder/compression weights.
