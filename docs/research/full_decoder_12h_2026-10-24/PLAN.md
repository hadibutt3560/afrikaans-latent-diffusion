# Full decoder experiment: a measured 6–12-hour allocation

Fixed on 24 October 2026 before the run, this experiment compared fine-tuning all 12 mT5 decoder blocks plus the final layer norm (F12) with fine-tuning only the last two blocks (C2), both starting from the unchanged autoencoder (job 56531, step 30500) with the encoder, Perceiver, embeddings and diffusion frozen, the same data order (seed 20261024 + epoch) and a 12-hour allocation on one RTX 8000. Checkpoints were selected by mean sentence chrF on the 81 previously inspected development passages, and reserve admission required at least +3 chrF and at least 14/81 exact reconstructions (F12 also at least +1 chrF over C2). Success on the 103-passage reserve required +3 chrF with a positive 95% interval from 10,000 paired document-bootstrap replicates, at least +3 percentage points of exact reconstruction and no fidelity or degeneracy regression.

The plan also set the clock-based budget and abort rules, crash handling, the conditional unconditional-generation transfer on 256 shared latents (seeds 20261024–20261279) with its regression guards, and the export and reporting rules. The full plan as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/full_decoder_12h_2026-10-24/PLAN.md.
