# Diffusion latents against simpler latent priors

Fixed on 30 October 2026 before the run, this inference-only study compared validation outputs of the trained diffusion models (five small and five large runs, 1,000 rows each) with outputs decoded through the fixed B0 decoder from three simpler latent sources of 1,000 latents each: isotropic (C1, seeds 30000–30999), a projected Gaussian fitted with Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage (C2, seeds 40000 + i) and recombined training slots (C3, seeds 50000 + i). The two primary contrasts were, for each size, the mean length-standardised mGPT perplexity of the five runs minus that of C3 (band weights 0.140, 0.223, 0.349, 0.288), each with an approximate t-based 97.5% interval (t₄ = 3.4954) and a within-band control bootstrap of 10,000 replicates (seed 20261030). There was no training and no test-split access, and the reserve documents (positions 64–127 of the seed-91427 shuffle) were excluded from new passage-level evaluation.

The protocol also fixed the human reference conditions, the diversity and overlap readouts, the outcome wording, the reproduction and budget gates, an optional Phase B on per-slot normalisation of sampled latents, and the diffusion bank hashes. The full protocol as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/latent_controls_2026-10-30/PROTOCOL.md.
