# Literature search record, 1 November 2026

Supports two manuscript statements:
- Introduction: "We found no study that tests whether its autoencoder preserves passages when adapted with limited in-domain data".
- Related work: "We found no evaluation of diffusion-based generation for Afrikaans".

Both are worded as "we found no", not "none exists". The search was moderate, not exhaustive.

## Method

- **Who ran it:** an AI research assistant.
- **Tool:** general web search, about nine searches, with the results followed up in the ACL Anthology, arXiv, OpenReview, NeurIPS proceedings and PMLR.
- **Exact query strings were not logged.** The topics searched were:
  - diffusion text generation for Afrikaans;
  - diffusion language models for African and low-resource languages;
  - latent text diffusion, and autoencoder reconstruction or fidelity, in low-resource or multilingual settings;
  - multilingual discrete diffusion language models;
  - PLANNER and other latent-diffusion follow-ups in multilingual settings.

## Findings

### (i) Diffusion-based generation evaluated for Afrikaans

Nothing found.

Closest work:
- Autoregressive generation for African languages, such as Cheetah (ACL 2024). None of it uses diffusion.
- A diffusion-based translation submission to WMT 2024, covering English to Russian, German, Czech and Spanish. No Afrikaans.
- A masked diffusion model for Turkish (Diffutron). Seen in results, not read.

### (ii) Autoencoder fidelity in latent text diffusion for a new language or limited data

Nothing found.

Closest work:

- Zhang et al. (PLANNER, NeurIPS 2023): discusses decoding errors. English only.
- Meshchaninov et al., "Cosmos" (arXiv 2506.21170): latent-space design. English only.
- DiLaDiff (arXiv 2605.23605): seen only as a search snippet; not read.
