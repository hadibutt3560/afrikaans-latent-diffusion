# Train-versus-held-out reconstruction diagnostic

Fixed on 27 October 2026 before the run, this inference-only diagnostic tested whether the original autoencoder (job 56531, update 30,500) reconstructed its own training passages materially better than held-out ones, which would point to a failure to generalise rather than to fit, capacity or optimisation. Panel V took up to four passages per document from validation positions 128–250 of the seed-91427 shuffle (about 194 passages, none over 128 tokens), and panel T took at most one training passage per document with the same length-bin counts (seed 91430); decoding used the benchmark settings (beam 4, max length 128, no_repeat_ngram_size 3), and the primary statistic was Δexact, the model-view exact-match rate on T minus that on V, with 10,000 document-bootstrap replicates (seed 91431). The outcome was generalisation-limited if exact_T ≥ 3 × exact_V, the lower 95% bound of Δexact was at least +10 percentage points and the cross-entropy gap lay above 0; fit-limited if exact_T ≤ 1.5 × exact_V and the upper bound of Δexact was at most +10 points; memorisation if the shuffled-latent gate failed; and otherwise indeterminate.

The protocol also fixed the input hashes, the shuffled-latent and greedy arms, the reproduction and integrity gates, the action following each outcome and a 2 GPU-hour cap. The full protocol as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/train_heldout_reconstruction_2026-10-27/PROTOCOL.md.
