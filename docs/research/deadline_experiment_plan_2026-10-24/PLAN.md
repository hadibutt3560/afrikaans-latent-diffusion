## Recommendation and evidence

Run one controlled **decoder adaptation experiment with the latent representation frozen**. Compare continued adaptation of the last decoder block against adaptation of the last two decoder blocks. Keep the original checkpoint as an unchanged reference. This is one intervention, with an additional-training control, not a parameter sweep.

The most directly demonstrated bottleneck is the complete reconstruction path. It is not established that the decoder alone causes the errors. On 81 validation passages, correct versus shuffled latents give CE 1.0790 versus 8.3189; correct-latent reconstruction has chrF 67.7005, 11 exact reconstructions and numeric recall 21/33. Clear content changes occur without input truncation. Evidence: [Stage1 results](C:/Users/SCIENTIST/Documents/GitHub/research/docs/research/reconstruction_audit_2026-10-22/RESULTS.md), [81 primary examples](C:/Users/SCIENTIST/Documents/GitHub/research/outputs/reconstruction-audit-20261022/examples.jsonl).

The original AE already trained the final decoder block and Perceiver components jointly. Adapting one additional decoder block while freezing all representation components tests whether the existing source information can be decoded more faithfully. It can improve the decoder applied to the existing diffusion checkpoints without changing the distribution those checkpoints were trained to model. It cannot recover information absent from the frozen representation. It also may overfit or make the decoder less suitable for diffusion-sampled latents; both are explicit failure possibilities.

Removing the repetition constraint already failed its gate (chrF +0.9185, CI -0.1104 to 2.0160; numeric recall -6.06 percentage points). Keep that constraint. Larger diffusion already improves MSE/PPL while sharing the same fidelity limitation; further scaling is lower value. A new tokenizer, wider latent representation or joint AE retraining would change the diffusion training space and require a much larger re-evaluation. No such change belongs in this deadline experiment. See [Stage3](C:/Users/SCIENTIST/Documents/GitHub/research/docs/research/stage3_improvement_2026-10-22/RESULTS.md) and [Stage4](C:/Users/SCIENTIST/Documents/GitHub/research/docs/research/stage4_final_evaluation_2026-10-23/FINAL_EVALUATION.md).

The architectural rationale is consistent with the separation of autoencoding and latent generation in [Lovelace et al. (2023)](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/b2a2bd5d5051ff6af52e1ef60aefd255-Abstract-Conference.html), and the inherited decoder comes from [mT5](https://aclanthology.org/2021.naacl-main.41/). Neither paper establishes that this proposed local intervention will work. The recommendation is a project-specific hypothesis, not a literature-backed guarantee.

## Selection, stopping and resource budget

Use the **81 already inspected Stage1 validation passages** as the development panel; use the existing **103-passage/64-document reserve once** after selection. Source IDs are in [the Stage1 manifest](C:/Users/SCIENTIST/Documents/GitHub/research/outputs/reconstruction-audit-20261022/manifest.json). The reserve was not consumed by Stages1–4, but the validation corpus has historical exposure. It is a disjoint prospective confirmation panel for this intervention, not an untouched dataset. Do not inspect its outputs for tuning or select a second checkpoint after seeing its result.

An eligible development checkpoint must have no empty/capped reconstructions, numeric precision and recall no more than two percentage points below B0, and mean within-output repeated-word-trigram fraction no more than 0.005 above B0. Within each arm choose highest mean sentence chrF among eligible checkpoints, breaking ties by greater exact reconstruction, then earlier step. The unchanged step0 checkpoint is eligible. Cross-entropy and external PPL do not select checkpoints.

For admission to reserve confirmation require a development gain of at least three chrF points over B0 and at least three additional exact reconstructions (14/81 or better), with the above guardrails. T2 must also beat the best eligible C1 by at least one chrF point to support expansion to a second block. Otherwise C1 may be the single candidate if it passes the B0 gate; this is the within-experiment fallback, not a second sweep. Fix one candidate before reserve evaluation. If neither passes, retain B0 and end the investigation.

Advance arms in matched500-update blocks, alternating arms on one GPU. Use the same planned update ceiling and cumulative passage order; do not give the apparent winner extra training. Report each arm's selected step and compare same-step results as well as best eligible checkpoint results. Best-checkpoint differences do not identify an equal-update causal effect by themselves.

After at least1,000 updates/arm, stop both if neither arm improves its best eligible chrF by at least0.5 points over two consecutive checks (i.e.1,000 updates without that progress). Immediately abort an arm for nonfinite loss/gradients, changed frozen weights or misaligned inputs; stop both on the same resource boundary if paired continuation cannot be completed. Two consecutive checks with a numeric-recall fall exceeding five percentage points or repeated-output fraction above0.02 stop that arm for degeneration. Failed arms remain reported. Do not repair them by changing LR, precision, tokenizer or decoding during the run.

**Budget:** target at most18 GPU-hours for the two training arms together, leaving up to6 hours within a24-hour experiment window for loading, validation, checks and confirmation. This is a budget ceiling, not a measured duration. This is workload sizing, not hyperparameter search. If even1,000 updates per arm plus evaluation cannot fit, do not train.

These are prospective pragmatic thresholds for this experiment, not revised thresholds for the proposal's original hypothesis.
