# One bounded experiment before 27 October 2026

This plan, written on 24 October 2026 as a proposal before any launch, set one decoder adaptation experiment with the latent representation frozen: a control (C1) training only the last mT5 decoder block and final layer norm against a treatment (T2) also training the penultimate block, both from the unchanged autoencoder (job 56531, step 30500), with fresh AdamW (learning rate 2e-5), one shared data permutation (seed 20261024) and at most 3,000 updates per arm. Checkpoints were chosen by mean sentence chrF on the 81 Stage 1 development passages, and reserve admission required at least +3 chrF and at least 14/81 exact reconstructions over B0 (T2 also at least 1 chrF above C1). The candidate would count as a confirmed reconstruction improvement on the 103-passage reserve only with at least +3 chrF and a paired 95% interval excluding zero (10,000 document-bootstrap replicates, seed 20261025), at least +3 percentage points of exact reconstruction, numeric recall and precision falling by no more than 2 percentage points, and no worsening in the researcher's error counts.

The plan also recorded the hardware checks, the budget (at most 18 GPU-hours for training) and stopping rules, the gated transfer check on 256 shared latents (seeds 20261024–20261279) with its regression guards, and the schedule up to submission. The full plan as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/deadline_experiment_plan_2026-10-24/PLAN.md.
