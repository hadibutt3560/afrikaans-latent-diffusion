# Autoencoder recipe, extra data and latent length: screen and diffusion

Fixed on 27 October 2026 before the runs, this screen tested whether retraining the autoencoder with the published full-mT5 recipe improved reconstruction over the current autoencoder (job 56531, B0), whether about 511k extra words of government-domain Afrikaans or 64 latent slots helped, and whether diffusion on the best new autoencoder improved unconditional generation. Four arms (A and B on NCHLT, C and D on NCHLT plus the extra data, each with 32 × 64 or 64 × 64 slots) were trained once with seed 20261027, ranked by selection-panel cross-entropy (positions 0–63 of the seed-91427 validation shuffle), with the 32-slot arm preferred if within 2%, and diffusion ran only if the winner beat B0. The primary reconstruction result was winner minus B0 on the reserve panel (positions 64–127), counted as an improvement only with at least +10 percentage points of exact match and +3 chrF, both with 95% lower bounds above 0 (paired document bootstrap, 10,000 replicates, seed 20261028), and numeral recall no more than 0.02 below B0.

The protocol also fixed the data and panels, the training recipe, the diffusion settings, the random-latent control, the generation criteria against the existing benchmark, the job budgets and the reporting rules. The full protocol as fixed before the run is at the repository tag `as-run`: https://github.com/hadibutt3560/afrikaans-latent-diffusion/blob/as-run/docs/research/ae_screen_2026-10-27/PROTOCOL.md.
